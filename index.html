🔍 About the Research

This research focuses on assessing attention span and fluctuation levels in children diagnosed with ADHD using EEG signals and machine learning. We leverage both time and frequency domain features, including ERP components, to uncover attention patterns.

📊 Dataset & Methodology

- EEG signals collected using a 60-channel BrainAmp amplifier. - Visual stimuli were used to elicit ERP responses. - Feature extraction using wavelet transform & ERP analysis. - Machine learning models trained to identify attention patterns.
